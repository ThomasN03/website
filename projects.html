Food Forward Delivery App-
Food Forward plays a vital role in delivering healthy, partially prepared meal kits to low-income families in Duluth, helping to address food insecurity while supporting community well-being.
The key issue that needed to be solved for them was their delivery process. Our project was designed to take routes of various stops and provide easy step by step directions for their drivers


Fitness App-
This fitness app allows you to log events you want to schedule and then select a location for the even. You can then get directions to the event from you location.
